# Implications for AI Governance & Verification

## Key Challenges:
- How can centralized AIs verify decentralized systems?
- How should verification protocols adapt to local-first architectures?
- What stability concerns arise from cross-model influence?

## Local-First Implications:
Local systems:
- Enhance privacy  
- Reduce dependency on cloud providers  
- Increase user autonomy  
- Complicate regulatory oversight  

## Conclusion:
AI governance must evolve to account for:
- Decentralized architectures  
- Independent developers  
- Model interoperability
